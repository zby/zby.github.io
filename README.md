## Current Focus

I am building [Commonplace](https://zby.github.io/commonplace/), a self-hosting
LLM wiki for turning AI-assisted work into durable, inspectable, linked
knowledge.

The broader question I am exploring is whether AI-assisted thinking can
accumulate: not just chat transcripts, but reusable claims, sources, procedures,
reviews, and open loops that other people and agents can inspect.
