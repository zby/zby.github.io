This is my [Public Mind Interface](https://github.com/Open-Research-Institute/public-mind-template):
a compact map of the work I want other minds to understand, challenge, reuse,
or join.

My current frontier is **agent-operated knowledge bases**. I am building
[Commonplace](https://zby.github.io/commonplace/), a self-hosting LLM wiki:
the theory of how LLM wikis should work lives inside the wiki, and LLM agents
execute that theory to maintain the wiki itself.

The practical question behind this work is simple: can we make AI-assisted
thinking accumulate? Chat produces insight that scrolls away. A public mind
should turn good fragments into durable claims, linked evidence, reusable
procedures, and visible open loops.

## Current Open Loops

- [Commonplace](https://zby.github.io/commonplace/) - the self-hosting LLM wiki and knowledge-base methodology.
- [Epistack Competition](./epistack-competition/) - using Commonplace on epistemic case studies.
- [llm-do](./llm-do/) - currently on hold while I focus on Commonplace as the more basic exploration.
